# Leone Lens — The 10 First Questions

Ask these before any build. Record the answers. A missing answer is a blocker, not a detail.

1. What outcome — not task, not use case — are we buying, and how will we measure it in money, time, quality or risk?
2. Who owns that outcome by name, and what decision rights are we delegating to the system?
3. What are the policies, risk limits, approval thresholds and escalation boundaries — written down, before we build?
4. Why is this not embedded in Fusion? What data, security or workflow are we rebuilding outside it, and why?
5. Which steps truly need LLM reasoning, and which are deterministic computation we should never pay tokens for?
6. Assist, research, or governed action — and what evidence earns the next notch on the autonomy dial?
7. What does the Outcome Receipt need to show for Finance, HR, or Audit to sign off?
8. What is the AI-Unit budget per outcome, the kill switch, and the model-downgrade path?
9. Are we using an Oracle prebuilt, Marketplace validated agent, or Agent Studio template first, before building custom?
10. For implementation work: are we using FSCR as the authority, with agents as operators and experts as approvers — or recreating setup knowledge project by project?
