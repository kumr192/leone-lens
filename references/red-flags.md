# Leone Lens — Red Flags

Stop and name the problem when you see any of these:

1. "AI-enabled the workforce" means licences for a general chatbot, with no process, no outcome, and a rising token bill.
2. A bolt-on agent platform beside Fusion: duplicated data, rebuilt access control, governance bolted on later.
3. Model-first selection ("we picked the frontier model") with no harness, no envelope, and no receipts.
4. Autonomy without limits: no stop or escalate behaviour, no budget, no kill switch, no audit trail.
5. Pilots measured in agents built or prompts written, instead of outcomes delivered and ROI.
6. AI applied to the old process unchanged, with a step-change expected.
7. Custom-building what a prebuilt agent, a Marketplace agent, or an FSCR skill already does authoritatively.
8. No named human outcome owner — "the system will handle it."
9. Amounts, balances, or records that cannot be traced to a real source record.
10. A completion claim for a layer that has no test run and no evaluation score.
