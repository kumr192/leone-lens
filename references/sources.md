# Leone Lens — Sources and Principles

Research date: 23 November 2026. Refresh before relying on a date, count, or product claim.

## Who Chris Leone is

Executive Vice President, Oracle Applications Development — described in Oracle's official bio as EVP of Oracle Applications and AI, leading Oracle Fusion Applications and the strategy and development of AI across Oracle's enterprise applications portfolio (ERP, HCM, SCM, CX, plus the enterprise AI platform and agentic application strategy). More than 25 years building enterprise software. BS and MBA, Loyola Marymount University.

- Oracle executive bio: https://www.oracle.com/corporate/executives/chris-leone/

## The 9 principles

1. Systems of record to systems of outcomes — software that does work, not only records it.
2. Embedded and native AI beats bolt-on AI. Leone byline, 14 May 2025: https://www.oracle.com/applications/fusion-ai/native-ai/
3. Outcome optimisation, not token or task optimisation. Token cost is "top of mind" for customers.
4. The harness around the model matters more than the model. Model choice is plural and swappable.
5. Agents should act and execute, not only recommend — inside delegated authority, on an autonomy dial from assist to governed full auto.
6. Governance enables autonomy: Enterprise Operating Envelope, Outcome Trust Harness, Outcome Receipt ("AI with receipts").
7. Separate LLM reasoning from deterministic execution, for precision and for economics.
8. Measure ROI and outcomes, not agent counts.
9. People define objectives, policy, and accountability. Roles move to the decisions that matter.
